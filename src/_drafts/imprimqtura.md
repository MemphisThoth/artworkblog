# Imprimatura

An imprimatura is the first layer of thin paint on your canvas or wood panel. Painters like ot use it to paint on a surface that isn't white. 

When you paint on a white support, all of the colors appear darker. The imprimatura allows you to judge values, the light and dark, of your painting. 

When you work on a toned surface, you can work both dark to light and light to dark. 

Painters tone the surface with a mixture of burnt sienna and a solvent. I recommend [Gamblin Gamsol](https://gamblincolors.com/oil-painting/gamsol/). You make a thin wash like you would in watercolor. 

Other painters use acrylic paints or toned gesso for the first layer.

You can use other colors too. 

Also, a bistre technique is where you rub out the highlights in your imprimatura.

So, I would encourage you to try the imprimatura method in your next painting.
